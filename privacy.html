Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Overview

Ragequarium Automation is a privately operated application used to manage video uploads for the Ragequarium YouTube channel.

Information Accessed

With the operator's authorization, the application accesses YouTube account information and permissions necessary to upload and manage videos.

How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to authenticate the operator, verify the connected YouTube channel, and perform authorized video management operations.

Data Storage

OAuth credentials and authorization tokens are stored locally on the operator's computer and are not published or sold to third parties.

Data Sharing

The application communicates with Google's YouTube API to perform authorized operations. It does not sell personal information.

Revoking Access

The operator may revoke the application's access through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application operator through the contact information provided on the Ragequarium YouTube channel.
